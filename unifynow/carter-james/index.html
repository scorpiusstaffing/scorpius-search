Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What roles do you focus on?

SaaS Sales roles, from the first seller to the revenue leader: SDR and BDR, Account Executive, Sales Engineer and Solutions Consultant, Customer Success and Account Management, Partnerships, RevOps, Sales Team Lead, Head of Sales, VP Sales and CRO.

Do you only work with SaaS and tech companies?

Yes. It’s not a limitation, it’s our edge.

What does “candidate first” actually mean?

We don’t force every search through the same rigid process. We adapt to what your search actually needs, and we skip what doesn’t add value.

What if we already have internal candidates?

Good. We can help you assess them honestly, benchmark against external options, or simply validate that you already have the right person.

What geographies do you cover?

The Netherlands and Germany primarily. Our network runs deep in the Benelux and DACH SaaS ecosystems.
