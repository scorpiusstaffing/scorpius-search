The roles

Every seat in the revenue team.

Seven role families, set out in the order revenue moves: from the first conversation with a prospect to the people who answer for the number.

  1. 01

    Open

    • Sales Development Representative
    • Business Development Representative

    The start of the line.

    Sales and Business Development Representatives open conversations with the right accounts and turn interest into qualified pipeline. The good ones are curious, organised and hard to discourage.

  2. 02

    Prove

    • Sales Engineer
    • Solutions Consultant

    The technical half of the deal.

    Sales Engineers and Solutions Consultants run discovery, demos and proofs of concept. They show a buyer that the product does what the pitch says it does, and they make a claim believable.

  3. 03

    Close

    • Account Executive

    The person who owns the deal.

    Account Executives take an opportunity from first meeting to signature: discovery, business case, negotiation, close. SMB, mid-market and enterprise are three different jobs, so the brief starts with which one you need.

  4. 04

    Keep

    • Customer Success Manager
    • Account Manager

    Revenue you keep.

    Customer Success Managers and Account Managers get customers live, renewed and expanding. In a subscription business the sale is not finished at the signature. It comes round again.

  5. 05

    Extend

    • Partnerships

    Reach beyond your own team.

    Partnership roles build the reseller, integration and referral relationships that bring in deals your own sellers would not reach on their own.

  6. 06

    Run

    • RevOps

    The system behind the number.

    RevOps owns process, tooling, data and forecast, so the team spends its time selling and the number on the board is one you can believe.

  7. 07

    Lead

    • Sales Team Lead
    • Head of Sales
    • VP Sales
    • CRO

    The people who own the number.

    From the first Team Lead to the Chief Revenue Officer: the leaders who set the motion, hire the team and answer for the forecast. If it’s a hire that shapes your next phase of growth, it’s probably a fit.

How we work

A pragmatic approach to sales hiring.

Every search is shaped by the situation: your needs, your timeline, your context. Three steps, and we skip whatever doesn’t add value.

  1. Step 01

    We listen

    You share what you need: the role, the context, what success looks like. We share what we already know about the market and who’s in it.

  2. Step 02

    We move

    If we know the right candidates, we go straight to conversations. If the search requires more work, we do exactly what’s needed.

  3. Step 03

    You hire

    You meet exceptional, relevant candidates, make a confident decision, and get back to building your scale-up with the right people.

What we hold true

Community-driven

Outcome-driven

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What founders and candidates ask before we start.

What roles do you focus on?

SaaS Sales roles, from the first seller to the revenue leader: SDR and BDR, Account Executive, Sales Engineer and Solutions Consultant, Customer Success and Account Management, Partnerships, RevOps, Sales Team Lead, Head of Sales, VP Sales and CRO. If it’s a hire that shapes your next phase of growth, it’s probably a fit.

Do you only work with SaaS and tech companies?

Yes. It’s not a limitation, it’s our edge. We know the market, we know the people, and we know what good looks like in this space.

What does “candidate first” actually mean?

It means we don’t force every search through the same rigid process. We don’t add steps to justify fees. We adapt to what your search actually needs, and we skip what doesn’t add value.

What if we already have internal candidates?

Good. We can help you assess them honestly, benchmark against external options, or simply validate that you already have the right person. Not every search needs to be a full search.

What geographies do you cover?

The Netherlands and Germany primarily. Our network runs deep in the Benelux and DACH SaaS ecosystems.
