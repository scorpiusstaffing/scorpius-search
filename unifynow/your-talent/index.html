Your talent

Because of our specialization and approach, we are capable of fulfilling any executive search for you in addition to the regularly recurring positions within no time.

01

Recruitment &
Executive Search

Due to the fact that the market for recruitment & sales professionals happens to be considered as one of the most candidate driven markets at this moment, we truly believe that the key to our success revolves around the fact that we remain in touch with our candidates on a continuous basis. This way we don’t just get to the ins and outs of what’s happening in the market, we also make sure to be the first to be aware of any potential candidate being open for their next challenge. Because of our specialization and approach, we are capable of fulfilling any executive search for you in addition to the regularly recurring positions within no time.

02

Training &
Coaching

Because we are convinced that going that extra mile for our candidates and clients is another significant contributor to our success in conducing mutual beneficial and sustainable relationships between our candidates and clients, we provide Training & Coaching as part of our recruitment service.

However, for those clients that seek to further develop and optimize their recruitment and sales professionals’ expertise and experience, we also provide tailor-made training & coaching sessions that are fully targeted to your needs.

03

Consultancy

Because we as UNIFY NOW are specialized in recruitment & sales professionals, we conduct business with more than 20 agencies and hundreds of professionals on a weekly basis. As a result of numerous meetings and deep conversations with all sorts of agencies and professionals, we have developed an incomparable understanding of how recruitment is being conducted most effectively. During our consultancy sessions, we provide you with feedback on your approach, strategy, business, results, and plans, in order to come up with an action plan on how to run your business more effectively, optimize your results and achieve your goals.
