Corporate Recruiter

Salary

€50.000 - €65.000

Location

  • Amsterdam
  • Utrecht
  • Rotterdam
Apply

Are you done with KPI’s and ready for a challenging and flexible corporate recruitment role?

On behalf of a renowned consultancy firm, we happen to be exclusively searching for a Corporate Recruiter who endeavors to become end-responsible for the entire recruitment process from A-Z for multiple business units. In this role, you will not only be responsible for the recruitment process but also for the marketing activities and performance analysis.

Client

This consultancy firm is regarded as an outright top employer, due to its flexibility, secondary benefits and award-winning training & development plan. In this role, you will be even able to work 2-3 days from home since you will be fully responsible and accountable for your own business.

Responsibilities

  • Engage with hiring managers to define recruitment needs and plan the hiring process
  • Gather market intelligence and develop a solid understanding of the local marketplace
  • Define recruitment sourcing strategies to develop a pool of potential candidates
  • Organize hiring events and participate in job fairs to network with potential candidates
  • Source candidates on job boards, resume databases, professional networks and through referrals
  • Interview candidates at various stages (phone calls, video chats, and face to face meetings)
  • Drive the end-to-end recruitment process
  • Set hiring goals (e.g. quarterly and annual)
  • Track recruitment KPI’s, like time to hire, source of hire and time to fill
  • Execute the hiring and placement of successful candidates into the company

Requirements

  • Ability to understand business strategies and develop executive recruitment solutions
  • At least 1 year of recruitment experience
  • At least a Bachelor Degree (Human Resources or BA is preferred)
  • Strong interviewing skills and familiarity with various interview techniques
  • Experience with social media recruiting
  • Exceptional communication skills

On offer

  • Career at an outright A-Brand which is recognized as top employer for the past 3 years
  • Indefinite contract with a competitive base salary
  • Highly competitive commission scheme
  • Flexibility to determine own working hours + work 3 days from home
  • Lease car or €600,- compensation + Macbook + Iphone + pension & holiday allowances
  • Ongoing training and support
