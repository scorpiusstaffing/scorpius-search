Traineeship

Location

Apply

For a top tier executive search agency, we are currently exclusively recruiting for commercially driven, disciplined and ambitious trainees. This traineeship will take place in London and will be one of the best experiences in your life if you endeavor to become an expert in sales & recruitment.

Client

This executive search agency happens to not only be considered as a great employer because of its amazing working environment, personal management style and favorable working hours, it also seems to offer unlimited career opportunities, ongoing training & development, and a great atmosphere. Even the board of directors all started their career either as Trainees or as Junior Consultants. The key to this company’s success, therefore involves its organic growth and retention! They also offer a highly competitive base salary with a great performance-based commission scheme which amounts up to 30%!

Responsibilities

After the immense training of 4 weeks in London, you will become an officially qualified recruitment consultant. The initial two months you will start by researching your market and developing your network to become an expert in your field. These initial efforts are indispensable to be able to obtain a portfolio of valuable professionals. As soon as you are ready, you will become responsible for managing the entire recruitment process from acquiring new clients, maintaining the relationship but also fulfilling their needs on an operational level, which involves conducting job intakes, screening candidates, arranging interviews and ultimately make those deals take place!

Requirements

In order to be able to succeed in this role we expect you to at least have some commercial experience, a passion for recruitment, but also to have a natural talent for connecting with people. Since successful consultants happen to possess excellent interpersonal skills, a strong work ethic and commercial acumen, we only consider you for this traineeship if you believe that you have got what it takes!

On offer

  • Global relocation to for instance New York, Singapore, Sydney, Berlin & Amsterdam
  • Opportunity to grow within the first year into a qualified 360 consultant role
  • Highly competitive uncapped commission in addition to relatively high base salary
  • Award-winning in-house training and development for every stage of your career
  • Professional, supportive and sales driven working environment
