Six ways to fuse a U and an N.

Each concept is shown dark on light and light on dark: the monogram, a horizontal and a stacked lockup, and the favicon at 32 and 16 pixels. The wordmark is the same in all six so that only the mark is being compared.

Back to the site

01Line

A capital U and a capital N drawn as one stroke: the right stem of the U is the left stem of the N.

02Turn Recommended, used on the site

A u turned half a circle is an n. Joined on the stem they share, the two letters become one continuous line.

03Stack

One glyph instead of two: the stems are shared, the N sits on its diagonal in the upper half and the U closes the form below.

04Cut

A solid square with two opposing cuts. The cut from above leaves a u, the cut from below leaves an n.

05Clasp

The u and the n stay two pieces that hook into each other, like two links of a chain.

06Trace

The single line of concept 02 drawn as a circuit trace: 45 degree chamfers and a terminal at each end.
