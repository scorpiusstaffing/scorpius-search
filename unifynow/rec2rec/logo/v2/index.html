Unify Now · Logo, round 2

Six refinements of the shared stem.

All six keep what was chosen: a capital U and a capital N on one stem, the bold geometric weight, the rounded tile in both polarities and the tracked capitals beneath it. Each is shown on the tile at 256, 64, 32 and 16 pixels, as bare letters, and in the horizontal and stacked lockup.

01Optical Recommended

Concept 01 corrected: the diagonal is 12 percent thinner than the stems so all three weights read as equal, the bowl overshoots the baseline, and the letters sit on the optical centre of the tile.

02Single stroke

One line of one weight: the bowl of the U runs on into the stem of the N, bends into the diagonal and bends again into the last stem. No joins, two round bends.

03Terminals

The optical version with the two free tops cut at 45 degrees in the direction of the diagonal: the first and the last stroke rhyme with the one in the middle.

04Squircle

The optical letters on a tile with continuous curvature instead of a plain radius, and set four percent larger in it.

05Negative

The optical letters cut out of the tile, so the ground shows through them: one file that works on any background. It has no bare form; the tile is the mark.

06Outline

The single-stroke construction as a monoline outline for large use (hero, print). The solid version stays the icon.

07Wordmark

Tracked capitals against sentence case, both set in the same typeface and weight.
