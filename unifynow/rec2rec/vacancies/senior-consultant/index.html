Vacancy 02 of 09

Senior Consultant

Salary
€50.000 - €60.000
Location
Amsterdam, Utrecht, London, Frankfurt, New York, Barcelona
ApplyAll vacancies

Are you done with being micromanaged on input and ready to run your own business?

For a renowned executive search agency, we are currently exclusively recruiting for a disciplined senior consultant that takes ownership and endeavors to become a successful top biller or leader. Within this company, you have the responsibility and flexibility to determine your own working hours, whatever you like to wear and even your own strategy on how to conduct business most effectively.

Client

Apart from the fact that this executive search agency happens to be well known for its reputation as a market leader in 3 distinctive markets, this client appears to be considered amongst recruiters as a wonderful employer to work for due to the fact that you, as a senior consultant, have the opportunity to start and run your own discipline, which is already proven to be successful in other countries. Moreover, in order to encourage its employees to develop sustainable relationships with its clients and candidates, unlimited access is being provided to the company’s credit card.

In terms of the career opportunities for you as a Senior Consultant, we would definitely consider this client one of the most favorable ones. In addition to the countless opportunities that arise when you start your own discipline, the fact that even the board of directors all started their career once at the bottom of the organization shows that anything is possible at this company in terms of career advancement.

Responsibilities

  • Develop a commercial understanding of the market, from both a client and candidate perspective
  • Build a strategy to maximize revenues including business development and account management
  • Act as an ambassador of the group in order to sustain and develop business relationships
  • Negotiate commercial terms with both key accounts as well as new clients
  • Manage the recruitment process from interview to offer stage, balancing excellent service with a positive commercial outcome for all parties
  • Provide consultative advice to clients and candidates and offer tailor-made solutions
  • Network in industry, sector, and specialism and become an expert in your own market
  • Sell the benefits to your customers of being exclusively represented by you and your company
  • Manage the customer’s expectations; clearly, articulate the proposition and service attributes
  • Work as an integral member of a team, motivating one another and communicating effectively
  • Provide guidance and mentor less-experienced consultants

Requirements

  • 1-3 years of recruitment experience at a reputable recruitment firm
  • Proven track record of billings within a similar market
  • Ability to get things done in the ‘right’ way, with ethics, integrity and positive intention
  • Able to establish a systematic course of action to effectively and efficiently achieve objectives
  • Willingness to make decisions, render judgments, and take action
  • Commercially inclined, independent and proactive

On offer

  • Supportive, flexible but sales driven working environment
  • Global relocation to for instance New York, Singapore, Sydney, Berlin & Amsterdam
  • Opportunity to grow within the first year into a Managing Consultant / Principal Consultant role
  • Highly competitive uncapped commission on both your own as well as your teams’ performance
  • Award-winning in-house training and development for every stage of your career
  • Lease car, company phone, laptop, holiday allowances, insurance plan and > 25 paid holidays
