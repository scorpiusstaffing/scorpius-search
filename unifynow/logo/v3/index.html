Unify Now · Logo, round 3

The same mark, drawn better.

Six drawings of the chosen idea: a capital U and a capital N on one shared stem, bold, on the rounded tile, with the tracked capitals. What changes is the drawing itself: the proportions of the two letters, the weight of the diagonal against the stems, the joints where the diagonal meets a stem, the bowl of the U, the terminals, and how the letters sit in the tile. Each is shown at 256, 64, 32 and 16 pixels on the tile, as bare letters and in both lockups, in both polarities.

01Round Recommended

Classic geometric proportions: U two thirds of its height, N a little wider, a true semicircular bowl with 1.5 percent overshoot, the diagonal 9 percent thinner than the stems and shaved thinner again where it meets them.

02Square

The same letters with the bowl as a superellipse: slightly squared, so the U answers the straight stems of the N and the corners of the tile.

03Tall

A narrower, taller pair: the letters stand almost square in the tile, with more height than width, as in a condensed geometric capital.

04Trap

The round letters with a visible ink trap at both joints of the diagonal: a small notch that keeps the crotch open at every size.

05Light

A lighter weight, stems one ninth of the height, the diagonal a touch thinner still, more air in the counters: the tile stays bold, the letters become refined.

06Wide

An extended pair with a shallower diagonal and a softly squared bowl: a lower, calmer mark that fills the tile from side to side.
