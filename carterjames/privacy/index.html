This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. Fonts, images and scripts are served from this website itself, so opening a page does not send your data to other parties.

Booking a meeting

The “Book a meeting” buttons open a Google Calendar appointment page. What you enter there is processed by Google under Google’s privacy policy, and reaches Carter James so the meeting can take place.
